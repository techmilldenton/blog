What TechMill Does

EDUCATIONAL WORKSHOPS

Whether you want to learn how to code, run marketing campaigns, or integrate technology into your business - we run a wide range of workshops throughout the year.

REGULARLY SCHEDULED

The key to building community is being consistent. Our calendar features not only unique events, but regular meetups that you can attend weekly and get to know the community.

ENTREPRENEUR SUPPORT

Freelancers, small businesses, startups, and entrepreneurs alike - we aim to provide resources and support for all shapes and sizes.

ONLINE / OFFLINE

It doesn't matter if you prefer to hang out in-person at our meetups, or online in our Slack group - there's always a way to meet your community and collaborate.

Soon we will be relaunching our newsletter as The Grist. This will be a great way to stay up-to-date on the latest happenings at TechMill. Readers will also have the opportunity to give us feedback.

The Grist will start arriving in your inbox on the second Monday of every month. That starts with this month, so be sure to sign up before the 8th! Our first issue will feature:

  * upcoming events
  * helpful tech tips
  * highlights from our website



You can sign up at the bottom of this post or in the sidebar of any of our blog posts. If there’s something you’d like to see in The Grist, please reach out to us. We’d love to hear from you. See you on the 8th!

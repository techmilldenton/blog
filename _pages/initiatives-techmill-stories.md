## What's YOUR TechMill Story?

We've found that a lot of people in our community have some great stories relating to their TechMill experience. Maybe you've found a job, a life partner, or just a new restaurant that you really like.

Whatever your TechMill story is, we'd like to hear it! Stories should be at least 50 words and show how TechMill has made a positive impact on your life (sorry, TechMill board members are ineligible). Valid entries will be entered into a drawing, and the winner will receive a gift certificate for a free t-shirt!

If you want to share your story with us, send an email with "TechMill Story" in the subject to techmilldenton {at} gmail {dot} com. Entries must be received by 11:59 PM Central Time on October 10, 2018.
